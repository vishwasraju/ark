# COBRA Overage Process

COBRA Overage

A COBRA overage process was put in place to keep the benefits plan in compliance with IRS guidelines. When COBRA overage processing runs on Sunday and Wednesday night, it evaluates all employees' benefits and their covered dependents.

When a dependent reaches his or her 23rd birthday (without the disabled indicator checked), the system automatically drops the dependent from any medical and/or dental coverage.

**Note:** Home Office changes the disabled indicator in the database once an dependent is approved per plan guidelines.
