# Corporate Resolutions

Corporate Resolutions

Formal resolutions may be made in one of these forms:

■ WHEREAS it is necessary to . . . ; and

■ WHEREAS conditions are such that . . . ; and

■ Therefore be it

■ RESOLVED, That . . . ; and be it

■ RESOLVED further, That . . .

Note that the word whereas is in caps with no comma following it; the first word after it is not capitalized unless it is a proper name.

The word resolved is also set in caps but is followed by a comma and a capital letter.
