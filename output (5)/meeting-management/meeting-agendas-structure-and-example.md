# Meeting Agendas: Structure and Example

MEETING AGENDA

A meeting agenda is a road map for a meeting. The agenda provides the plan for the meeting and a sense of direction and purpose.

Ameeting agenda should include (Figure 3.38):

■ Meeting starting and ending times

■ Meeting location

■ Topic headings with topic details

■ How much time each topic discussion is expected to last

■ Which meeting participants will facilitate the topics
